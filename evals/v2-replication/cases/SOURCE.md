# v2 replication — corpus source

40 fresh birth records sampled (seed 20261115) from
`vedastro-org/15000-Famous-People-Birth-Date-Location` (MIT), excluding every
chart used in `evals/v2/cases/`. Names dropped; local time + offset converted
to UTC; dashaflow computes every chart with Lahiri/whole-sign. No VedAstro
calculation or verdict is used.

Questions, clauses, grounds, tags, and rationale are project-authored. This
corpus was committed before any v2-replication arm output.
