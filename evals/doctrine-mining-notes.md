## Top 3 refusal triggers for the R4 refusal list

1. **Birth-time fragility ⇒ ascendant-dependent claims banned/flagged** (full-reading Step 1; the empty `birth-time-verification/` dir was presumably meant to hold this). `time_sensitivity` = borderline or fragile ⇒ all bhava/lagna-dependent interpretations must be flagged uncertain and birth-time verification advised. Falsifiable, and it gates every other house-based claim.
2. **Auspicious-verdict bans for elections** (muhurtha-analysis). Transit Moon in 3/5/7 Tara (Vipat/Pratyak/Vadha), or in 6/8/12 from natal Moon (Chandrabala), or in gandanta (±3°20′ water-fire junctions), or `kaal_sarpa.active` ⇒ the muhurtha must not be called auspicious, regardless of tool score. Fully mechanical, wrong answers are checkable.
3. **Undelivered-promise flags via avastha/delivery check** (full-reading, career-analysis, health-analysis, finance-analysis). Any yoga, career, health, or wealth claim made from a planet in Bala/Mrita avastha, combust, debilitated, or with `shadbala.percentage` < 80% — or stated without checking `shadbala.ishta_kashta_phala` — must be flagged as undelivered/diminished, not presented as a promise. This is the single most repeated grounding rule across the legacy set and covers the most common yes-man failure mode (confident claims from broken planets).
