# Public resources — what each free asset licenses

No human labels are purchased here. This inventory records what each public asset can and cannot
support in the zero-human screen.

| Resource | What it is | License / access | Licenses | Cannot license |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **OurNakshatra** | 602 expert-written concept Q&A pairs | CC-BY-4.0 | Terminology, item wording, coverage | Any chart- or person-specific claim; no ratings |
| **Jyotish-LLM** | 1M instruction rows over 200k synthetic charts, 15 hand-encoded rules | Apache-2.0 | Chart-fact fixtures, citation format, grouped-split method | Outcome truth; classical-text authority; real people |
| **BaziQA** | 555 expert-authored questions over 117 real charts, with keys and champion scores | Public repo | A design analogue: a divination system scored without a new panel | Vedic content; free-form reading quality |
| **CUET-PG Jyotish** | NTA “Jyotish – Falit” papers, 5 years, 4 official keys, Sanskrit medium | Public | Shastra-recall competence screen; hard-negative decoys | Chart interpretation; whole-reading quality |
| **BPHS / AstroCentral** | Classical rule corpus and worked example charts | Public / fair use | Rule text and example charts for rule-fidelity pilots | A rated benchmark; edition, notation and stage must be pinned |
| **JPL Horizons / Swiss Ephemeris** | Ephemeris and computation oracle | Free / open | Chart facts with declared tolerances | Anything interpretive |
| **AstroTalk / AstroYogi** | Marketplace listings with price, experience, order volume | Public | Cost benchmarks; practitioner stratification | Reading labels; consent for research use |
| **Forums / Reddit / YouTube** | Public reading traces and discussions | Public but no research consent | Adversarial examples; failure cases | An unbiased or consented label set |

## What is missing

The public record contains no **rated chart–person–text triple**: a specific computed chart, a
specific person or persona, and a specific reading, judged by someone competent. Each asset above
supplies at most two of the three legs. That is why the zero-human screen can test grounding,
competence, rule fidelity and consistency, but cannot test whether a jyotishi would call a reading
good.

## Adding a resource

Add one row, state the license, and name both what it licenses and what it cannot. Do not list a
resource as a label source unless it contains expert judgments of whole readings.
