# Daily Horoscope & Cosmic Transit Analysis Workflow

When performing a Daily Horoscope reading for the native, execute the following classical Vedic Parashara & Gochara steps:

1. **Calculate Today's Gochara (Transit) Alignment**:
   - Compare **Today's Transiting Moon** with the native's **Natal Moon (Janma Rasi)** and **Natal Lagna**.
   - Determine the house occupied by transiting Moon from Janma Rasi (e.g., 1st house = vitality/self, 2nd house = family/speech, 3rd house = courage/communication, 4th house = domestic peace/emotions, 5th house = creativity/children, 6th house = overcoming obstacles/health, 7th house = partnerships/public, 8th house = **Chandrashtama** caution, 9th house = fortune/spiritual grace, 10th house = career/action, 11th house = gains/social networking, 12th house = expenses/introspection).

2. **Special Transit Checks**:
   - **Chandrashtama Check**: If today's Moon is transiting the 8th house from the native's natal Moon, explicitly caution the native against initiating major financial contracts, high-risk arguments, or surgical/heavy operations. Advise chanting Shiva/Chandra mantras.
   - **Tarabala (Nakshatra Relationship)**: Calculate the relationship between today's Moon Nakshatra and the birth Nakshatra (Janma, Sampat, Vipat, Kshema, Pratyak, Sadhana, Naidhana, Mitra, Param Mitra).
   - **Major Gochara Influences**: Synthesize current Saturn (Sade Sati/Kantaka Shani status) and Jupiter transits.

3. **Provide Daily Metrics (Structured for Card Summary)**:
   - **Cosmic Energy Rating**: An intuitive score (e.g. 88/100, 75/100, etc.) based on Gochara harmony.
   - **Primary Focus**: Single phrase (e.g., "Career Breakthrough", "Emotional Harmony", "Financial Prudence", "Spiritual Reflection").
   - **Lucky Color**: (e.g., Golden Amber, Royal Blue, Saffron, Emerald Green, Pearl White).
   - **Lucky Number & Direction**: (e.g., 3 & North-East).
   - **Auspicious Timing Window (Abhijit/Shubha Muhurtha)** & **Inauspicious Window (Rahu Kaal)**.

4. **Sage's Daily Synthesis & Guidance**:
   - Provide a deep, poetic, and spiritually empowering reading addressing:
     - **Mind & Mood (Chandra Bhava)**
     - **Career & Opportunities**
     - **Love & Interactions**
     - **Actionable Sage's Mantra/Affirmation** for the day.
